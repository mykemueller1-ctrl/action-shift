## Rules that survive Saturday rush
- Labor % = labor dollars ÷ total net sales. Show the formula.
- Default CTap labor target on this seat = 28% (weekly tool). Study average was 29.1%.
- HOLD food cost unless the same day's invoices AND a count exist.
- Cooked food is not a void. Re-ring with promo/comp so the leak stays visible.
- Never invent a dollar. Never mix houses.
- Never ask for a schedule photo on the owner seat.
- One action per close. Management by exception.
- CTap POS today is PDQ. There is no public PDQ API. Paste or email ingest is the door.
- DoorDash eligible sales = Subtotal. Exclude tips, taxes, and customer fees.
- Documented 3P take = commission + merchant fees + ads/promos + error charges.
- Marketing spend is a choice. Do not bury it inside "the app fee."
